See it in action

Q: Why do plants need sunlight? (Year 5)
A: To make food by photosynthesis — plants turn sunlight, water and carbon dioxide into sugar and oxygen. 🌱
Q: Explain Newton's Second Law (Year 10)
A: Net force = mass × acceleration (F = ma). More force → more acceleration; more mass → less acceleration.
Q: Balance H₂ + O₂ → H₂O (Year 12)
A: 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O, so atoms balance on both sides. ⚗️
