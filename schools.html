Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can we get started?

Once payment is confirmed (or invoice issued), we set up your school account within 24 hours. Teachers and students can log in immediately.

Can we upgrade our package later?

Yes — you can upgrade to a larger package at any time and we'll prorate the difference.

Do students need to install anything?

No installation needed for the web version at ostazzai.com. Students can also download the OstazAI app on Android or iOS for mobile access.

Is there a free trial for schools?

Yes — we offer a 7-day free trial for schools. Contact us to set one up.

Can we pay by invoice / bank transfer?

Yes — select "Invoice / Bank Transfer" in the form and we'll send you a formal invoice. Annual billing is also available with a discount.

What subjects are covered?

All major subjects: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English, History, Geography, Computer Science, Economics, and more — depending on the curriculum.
