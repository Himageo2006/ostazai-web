Q: Solve 3x + 5 = 20
A: Subtract 5 from both sides → 3x = 15. Divide both sides by 3 → x = 5. ✅
Q: What is 35 + 27? (Year 2)
A: Add the tens: 30 + 20 = 50. Add the ones: 5 + 7 = 12. Then 50 + 12 = 62. ✅
Q: Find the area of a triangle with base 8 and height 5
A: Area = ½ × base × height = ½ × 8 × 5 = 20 square units. ✅
