This page explains how to request deletion of your account and of the data associated with it in OstazAI (أستاذ AI), published by IM NORTHSTAR LABS.

How to request deletion

  1. Email himageo2@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my account - OstazAI"
  3. Include the email address registered to your OstazAI account
  4. Deletion is completed within 30 days at the latest, and we email you a confirmation

You can also use the in-app feedback button 💬 inside OstazAI to send the request directly.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, email address and password
  • Chat history and any questions sent to the AI tutor
  • Usage statistics, points, streaks and bookmarks
  • Subscription records (card details are never stored by us)

What is kept, and for how long

No personal data is retained after deletion. Anonymised financial records (a payment transaction ID held by the payment gateway) may be kept solely to meet legal and accounting requirements. These cannot be used to identify you.
